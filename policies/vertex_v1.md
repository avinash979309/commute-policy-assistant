# Vertex Finance Transport Guidelines
Company: vertex
Version: 1

## 1. Eligibility
Company transport is available only to employees working in rotational or night shifts. Employees on general day shift (9:00 AM to 6:00 PM) are not eligible. There is no grade restriction. Home distance must be between 5 km and 30 km from the office.

## 2. Booking and Cut-off Timings
All bookings and changes for the next day must be made by 12:00 noon on the current day. Same-day changes are not allowed except in a medical emergency, which the transport desk handles case by case.

## 3. Night Shift Safety
Any drop scheduled after 9:00 PM is a night drop. A woman employee on a night drop always travels with a security escort, and the cab must be tracked live by the security control room. Women employees may also ask for an escort before 9:00 PM by calling the transport desk.

## 4. Weekend and Holiday Transport
There is no weekend or holiday transport. The only exception is a declared business-critical day, when the head of department may approve transport for named employees.

## 5. Ad-hoc and Airport Trips
Airport trips must be approved by the head of department and the finance controller. Vertex Finance does not offer any other ad-hoc trips.

## 6. Guest Travel
Guests may use a dedicated cab booked by the admin team. The booking must be made at least 48 hours in advance with the guest's name and phone number.

## 7. Cost Caps
A single airport trip may cost at most Rs 1,200. Total ad-hoc spend per employee is capped at Rs 6,000 per month.

## 8. No-show Policy
Every no-show is charged at Rs 200, deducted from the next salary. Three no-shows in a calendar month suspend company transport for the rest of that month.
