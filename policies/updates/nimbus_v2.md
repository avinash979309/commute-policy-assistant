# Nimbus Labs Employee Transport Policy
Company: nimbus
Version: 2

## 1. Eligibility
Company transport is available to employees in grades G1 to G5. Employees in grade G6 and above receive a monthly travel allowance instead and are not eligible for company cabs. An employee is eligible only if their registered home address is between 3 km and 35 km from the office. Interns are now eligible for all roster pickups and drops.

## 2. Booking and Cut-off Timings
Regular pickups and drops follow the published roster. Any change to a drop must be made at least 3 hours before the scheduled drop time. Any change to a pickup must be made by 8:00 PM on the previous day. Requests after the cut-off are not guaranteed and are handled by the transport desk only if a seat is free.

## 3. Night Shift Safety
A drop is a night drop if it is scheduled after 7:00 PM. On every night drop, a woman employee is accompanied by a security escort until she reaches home. A woman employee is never dropped first or picked up last on a night route without an escort on board. Drivers on night routes must share the live trip link with the employee.

## 4. Weekend and Holiday Transport
Saturday pickups and drops are available only with written approval from the reporting manager, submitted by 3:00 PM on Friday. There is no company transport on Sundays or on declared company holidays.

## 5. Ad-hoc and Airport Trips
An ad-hoc trip is any trip outside the roster. Airport trips are allowed only for official business travel and must be approved by the reporting manager before booking. Other ad-hoc trips need approval from the transport manager.

## 6. Guest Travel
Visiting guests may use company transport only if an HR business partner sponsors the visit and books the trip at least 24 hours in advance. Guests travel in a dedicated cab, never in a shared roster cab.

## 7. Cost Caps
A single ad-hoc or airport trip may cost at most Rs 1,800. Any amount above the cap is paid by the employee unless the department head approves the excess in writing.

## 8. No-show Policy
A no-show is when the employee is not at the pickup point within 5 minutes of the cab arriving, without cancelling. Two no-shows in a calendar month now suspend company transport for 7 days.
